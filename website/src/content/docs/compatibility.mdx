## Requirements used

Checked against the [upstream requirements](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot#what-you-need)
and [project status](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot#project-status) on **November 10, 2026**.

- **Game:** program `0400486024DEC000`, update v131072, build `e0f3ccfa`.
- **OS:** 64-bit Windows 10/11, or 64-bit Linux with glibc 2.35 or newer. The Linux AppImage also requires AVX2.
- **Graphics:** Vulkan 1.3 and mesh shaders with a current driver. Listed GPU families include GeForce RTX 20 series and newer, Radeon RX 6000 series and newer, and Intel Arc.
- **Memory and storage:** 16 GB RAM, 8 GB VRAM recommended, and about 60 GB free during the build.

Meeting these requirements does not guarantee a trouble-free run. AMD and Intel have known issues, and cards without
mesh shaders are unsupported even though a partial fallback exists. The checker does not maintain a driver-version
database; check the capabilities of your installed driver.
